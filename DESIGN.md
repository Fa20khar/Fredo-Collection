# Design System & Visual Guidelines — Fredo's Collection

## 1. Design Aesthetics & Visual Identity
Fredo's Collection adheres to a **luxury Pakistani fashion editorial aesthetic**. It evokes elegance, heritage craftsmanship, and trustworthy modern retail without generic AI cliches or flashy discount marketplace clutter.

## 2. Color Palette
| Token | Hex | Role | Usage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary Background** | `#FFFFFF` | 60% Canvas | Clean editorial backdrops, card bodies |
| **Secondary Background** | `#F8F6F2` | Warm Neutral | Section breaks, subtle card fills, drawer backdrops |
| **Primary Text** | `#171717` | High Contrast | Headings, essential titles, primary labels |
| **Secondary Text** | `#737373` | Muted Neutral | Descriptions, subtitles, metadata |
| **Primary Accent** | `#173D32` | Deep Emerald Green | Brand primary, CTAs, prices, active links |
| **Secondary Accent** | `#C6A36A` | Champagne Gold | Brand accents, badges, luxury highlights |
| **Borders** | `#E8E4DD` | Hairline Neutral | Subtle dividers, card borders |
| **Sale Accent** | `#B42318` | Crimson | Sale badges, discounted pricing alerts |
| **WhatsApp Accent** | `#25D366` | Official Brand Green | Direct chat actions, floating support button |

## 3. Typography
- **Headings & Display**: `Playfair Display` (Serif). Conveys sophistication, bespoke tailoring, and heritage Pakistani bridal/formal aesthetic.
- **Body & Prose**: `Plus Jakarta Sans` / `Inter` (Sans-Serif). Clean, highly legible, modern geometric proportions.
- **Numbers & Metrics**: `font-mono tabular-nums`. Ensures numerical tabular alignment across all prices and quantities.

## 4. Universal Anti-Slop Discipline
1. **Zero-Pill Metadata**: Metadata labels (e.g. `Embroidered Suits · Ladies`) are rendered as clean, unboxed text with subtle typographic separators (`·`), never generic rounded pill enclosures.
2. **Top Bar Contract**: Strictly adheres to 3 balanced zones (Zone 1: Wordmark `Fredo's Collection`, Zone 2: Single-line nav links, Zone 3: Search, Wishlist, Cart actions).
3. **Contrast Scrims**: Hero banner features a measured multi-stop linear gradient (`from-[#173D32]/95 via-[#173D32]/75 to-transparent`) ensuring minimum 4.5:1 contrast ratio over background imagery.
4. **Authentic Imagery**: High-fidelity photography specifically depicting Pakistani ethnic garments (embroidered Dhanak, luxury velvet shawls, Kurta Shalwar, tailored waistcoats).
